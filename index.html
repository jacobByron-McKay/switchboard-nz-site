Switchboard NZ

Ward and department numbers for New Zealand hospitals. Find the team you need, tap once, and the app dials the hospital and keys the extension for you. No switchboard queue.

Built for the people who need a ward number in a hurry, and who are usually standing somewhere with no signal when they do. The directory lives on the phone, so searching and dialling work whether or not you have bars.
