Switchboard NZ

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 September 2026

Switchboard NZ is a phone directory for New Zealand hospitals. It holds ward, department and team numbers, and dials them for you without going through the switchboard queue. This page says exactly what the app stores and where.

Switchboard NZ is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Health New Zealand | Te Whatu Ora.

What we collect

Three things, all given to us by you when you register:

  • Your work email address. It has to be a Health New Zealand or hospital address, because the directory is for hospital staff only. We send an eight digit code to it to check it is yours, and use it to identify your registration and contact you about it if we need to. We do not send marketing, newsletters or anything else to it.
  • Your role. The profession you choose, and a grade if you give one.
  • Your hospital. The site you selected when you registered.

We ask for your role and hospital because access to the directory is reviewed. We may confirm a registration with the hospital it names.

What stays on your phone and never reaches us

  • Searching the directory. Once the directory is on your phone, searching it happens on the phone and sends nothing anywhere.
  • Who you called, and when. Recent numbers and favourites are stored on the device only.
  • Calls and emails. The app hands a number to your phone's own dialler, or an address to your own mail app. It does not place calls itself and cannot see them.
  • Any edits you make to a contact.
  • Your location. If you allow it, location is used once, on the device, to put the nearest hospital at the top of the list. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and the app works without it.

Where the account data is held

Your email, role and hospital are stored in a database run by Supabase, in their Sydney, Australia region. Access is restricted so that an account can read and change only its own record.

The sign-in code is sent through Google's Gmail service. Google handles your address and the code to deliver it, and a copy of each code email stays in the sending account's Sent Mail, including after you delete your account. The code itself stops working ten minutes after it is sent. A short log of which addresses were sent a code is kept for seven days, to stop the sign-in email being abused.

The directory is downloaded to your phone after you sign in, and kept there encrypted so it works without an internet connection. Signing out or deleting your account removes it from the phone.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking software of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your details with anyone.
  • No reading of your contacts, photos, microphone or call history.

What we email you

Only the sign-in code, when you ask for one. There is no mailing list to be added to. The only other reason we would email you is a question about your own registration.

How long we keep it

Your record is kept while your account exists. Delete your account in the app, under Account, and the record and the sign-in are both removed straight away and for good.

Your choices

  • See what we hold: open Account in the app.
  • Correct it: email us, or delete the account and register again.
  • Delete it: Account, then Delete account.
  • Ask us anything about it using the address below.

Children

The app is made for hospital staff and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect, the date at the top changes and the app will say so at sign-in.
