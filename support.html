Support

Email jacobb334@gmail.com. Tell us which hospital you are at and we will usually come back the same day.

A number is wrong or missing

This is the most useful message you can send us. Give us the ward or team name, the extension you were given, and the hospital. You can also correct it yourself in the app: open the contact and tap Edit. That change is saved on your phone only, so send it to us as well and we will fix it for everyone in the next update.

The call rings the switchboard instead of the ward

The app dials the hospital's main line and then keys the extension for you, with a short pause in between. On some networks that pause needs to be longer. If the extension is not being picked up, tell us the hospital and the extension and we will lengthen it.

Registering

Registration is for hospital staff, so it needs your Health New Zealand or hospital work email. We send an eight digit code to that address, you type it into the app, then pick your role and hospital. There is no password.

If the code has not arrived after a few minutes, check your junk or quarantine folder, then tap Send it again. If your work address is refused and you think it should not be, email us the part after the @ and we will look at it.

The app is not finding a ward I know exists

Search matches the ward name and common abbreviations. Try the abbreviation on its own, for example CCU rather than Coronary Care Unit. If it is genuinely not there, send us the name and we will add it.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes, once you have signed in. The numbers are then stored on your phone, so searching and dialling work in a basement with no reception. Only the first sign-in needs an internet connection.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account and tap Delete account. Your registration and sign-in are removed for good.
